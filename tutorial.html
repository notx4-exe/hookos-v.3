Tutorial

A quick walkthrough of turning one idea into a complete reel blueprint.

01

Describe your idea

Head to the Generator and describe the topic you want a reel about. Be specific — “why students can't focus” works better than “productivity.”

YOUR IDEA
Why students can't focus
02

Choose a framework

Pick the hook framework that fits your topic: Curiosity Gap, Fear, Contrarian, Story, Statistics, Authority, Emotion, or Open Loop. Not sure which? Curiosity Gap and Story are the most forgiving starting points.

HOOK FRAMEWORK
Curiosity Gap
Story
Fear
Statistics
03

Watch it generate

HookOS writes your blueprint live — topic refinement, title, hook, script, scene plan, CTA, and metrics — each shown as soon as it's ready.

BLUEPRINT OUTPUT
✓ Hook
✓ Script
✓ Scene Plan
✓ CTA
04

Copy and film

Use the copy button on each card to grab exactly the part you need, and follow the numbered scene plan while filming.

YOUR WORKFLOW
Blueprint → Copy → Film → Edit
