COMMON QUESTIONS

Generation failed?

HookOS automatically retries any single part of a blueprint that fails to generate. If you still see an error after that, the AI provider may be temporarily unavailable — try again in a moment.

Do I need an account?

No — you can generate blueprints without signing in. Signing in with Google saves your blueprint history so you can come back to past generations.

How do I delete my account or data?

See our Data Deletion page for the available controls.
